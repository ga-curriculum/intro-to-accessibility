**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be familiar with the practices we can implement to provide accommodations to people with auditory disabilities.

## What are auditory disabilities?

Auditory disabilities can include deafness and hearing loss. People with audio disabilities may have difficulty hearing or understanding spoken language.

## Accommodating auditory disabilities

![An enthusiastic lightbulb character (named Wattson) with raised hands against a dark background is shown in a video player interface. The character's cheery demeanor emphasizes the importance of closed captioning for accessibility. The closed caption reads: Closed captioning provides an accessible experience!](./assets/closed-captioning.png)

We can use these techniques to accommodate people with auditory disabilities:

- Provide transcripts of audio content so that people who are deaf or hard of hearing can access the information.
- Use closed captioning for videos so that people who are deaf or hard of hearing can understand the audio content.
- Provide visual feedback when a user interacts with an element like a button.

### Who this benefits

Accommodating auditory disabilities benefits deaf and hard-of-hearing users but has benefits for others as well:

- Users that consume content in a location where playing audio would be inappropriate. Cultural norms vary, but many consider it rude to play audio in public settings. Providing a way for users who don't have headphones to consume the content without having to hear it in these situations can be helpful.
- Users who prefer to read text rather than listen to audio. Many people can read faster than spoken words can be communicated.
- Users who struggle with auditory recall. Many people struggle to retain information if it is only presented audibly. Having the same information presented in many ways can help information retention. Transcripts also help people consume information at their own pace.
- Users who don't have time to consume the entirety of a piece of audio content. Text can be searched easily, and highlights are easier to find.
- Users with temporary disabilities such as an ear infection.
- Users finding audio through a search engine. Transcripts allow search engines understand audio and video content.

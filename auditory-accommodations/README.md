**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be familiar with the practices we can implement to provide accommodations to people with auditory disabilities.

## What are auditory disabilities?

Auditory disabilities can include deafness and hearing loss. People with audio disabilities may have difficulty hearing or understanding spoken language.

## Accommodating cognitive disabilities

We can accommodate audio disabilities with accessibility by:

- Providing transcripts of audio content so that people who are deaf or hard of hearing can access the information.
- Using closed captioning for videos so that people who are deaf or hard of hearing can understand the audio content.
- Provide visual and audio feedback when a user interacts with an element like a button or link.

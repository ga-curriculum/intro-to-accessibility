# Intro to Accessibility - Cognitive Accommodations

![Hero image](./assets/hero.png)

**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be familiar with basic cognitive accomidations.

## What are cognitive disabilities?

Cognitive disabilities can include learning disabilities, intellectual disabilities, and autism spectrum disorder. People with cognitive disabilities may struggle with memory, attention, problem-solving, and communication.

## Accommodating cognitive disabilities

We can accommodate cognitive disabilities with accessibility by:

- Using clear and concise language and avoiding jargon when writing instructions and descriptions.
- Simplifying our interface so the choices aren't too busy, overwhelming, or confusing.
- Providing multiple ways for users to interact with our content, such as through text, audio, and video. This will give users more flexibility and allow them to choose the best way to meet their needs.
- Providing clear and concise feedback to users when they interact with our interface. This feedback can be visual, auditory, or haptic, and will help users confirm that their actions are having the desired effect.
